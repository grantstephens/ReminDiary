ReminDiary

An offline daily journal for Android and the web that shows you your own past. Write today's entry, save it, and it surfaces what you wrote on this same date in previous years.

What it is

  • One entry per calendar date
  • Saving today's entry reveals what you wrote on this day in past years
  • Everything lives on your device — no account, no sync
  • CSV export and import, so your writing is never locked in — see the import format & example
  • Ships with a single Android permission — see Privacy

Privacy

ReminDiary is built to work fully offline. The only network activity in the app at all is an opt-in, off-by-default analytics toggle in Settings. Read the full privacy policy for exactly what that does and does not send.
